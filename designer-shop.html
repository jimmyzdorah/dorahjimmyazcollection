/ GOOD TO KNOW /

Frequently Asked Questions.

Can I buy and hire in the same order?

Yes. Your bag can contain both purchase and hire items. Hire availability must be confirmed separately.

How do I choose my size?

Each product shows its available sizes. Contact us if you need sizing assistance.

How are hire prices calculated?

Hire estimates use the daily rate multiplied by the selected number of calendar days. Additional charges and deposits are confirmed separately.

Can I pay online?

Not yet. Checkout currently prepares a WhatsApp order request. Online payment integration will be added with the backend.

Do you offer delivery and returns?

Delivery arrangements, purchase returns and hire return conditions must be confirmed with the shop before your order is accepted.
